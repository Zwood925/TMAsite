**Hook:** When books became accessible, “regular people” stopped waiting for gatekeepers and started building. AI is the same shift—except it’s faster and cheaper.

## Why this matters now
- **Access flips outcomes.** Your team can research, draft, and prototype in hours—not weeks.
- **Winners move first.** Early adopters lock in advantages (data, workflows, audience trust).

## Do this this week (60–90 minutes total)
1. **Pick one friction** customers complain about (slow response, quotes, FAQs).
2. **Create a micro‑workflow**: intake → summarize → decide → reply. Keep it tiny.
3. **Ship a v0** using tools you already know (ChatGPT + Sheets + email).

## Tool picks
- **ChatGPT/GPT‑5** for reasoning and drafting.
- **n8n** to glue steps together (webhook → transform → email).
- **Google Sheets** as your lightweight database and audit log.

## What to avoid
- “We need to map *everything* before we start.” Nope. Ship one loop.
- Fancy stacks that slow you down. Velocity > elegance.

## The bottom line
This is a printing‑press moment. Don’t hire a poet—buy a press. Ship one useful loop this week, then repeat.
